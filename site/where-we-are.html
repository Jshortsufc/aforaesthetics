Where we are

Two Sheffield locations

You'll find Eleana at two welcoming Sheffield venues — one in the north of the city at Hillsborough, and one to the south on Abbeydale Road. Every appointment is by booking, and each starts with a free, no-obligation consultation.

Hillsborough · North Sheffield

A for Aesthetics at Peaches

Our home clinic sits inside Peaches, a friendly, well-established hair & beauty salon on Wadsley Lane in Hillsborough. It's a calm, private setting for treatments, easy to reach from across north Sheffield.

  • Address: 5 Wadsley Lane, Hillsborough, Sheffield S6 4EA
  • Best for: lip fillers, dermal fillers, skin boosters and more

Abbeydale Road · South Sheffield

A for Aesthetics at And Collective

We also treat clients at And Collective, a luxury beauty & wellbeing salon on Abbeydale Road in the south of the city — a stylish, relaxed space that's handy if you're based on this side of Sheffield.

  • Address: And Collective, Abbeydale Road, Sheffield S7 2BE
  • Best for: the same nurse-led treatments, on the south side of the city
