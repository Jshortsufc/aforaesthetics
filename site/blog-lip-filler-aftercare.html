Lip filler aftercare: what to expect

Written by Eleana, Advanced Nurse Practitioner & independent prescriber

Good aftercare makes a real difference to how your lips settle and how happy you are with the result. Here's a simple guide to the first few days.

The first 24 hours

Some swelling and tenderness is completely normal — lips are delicate and react more than most areas. Keep things gentle: avoid heavy exercise, alcohol, very hot drinks, saunas and makeup on the lips for the first day, and try to sleep slightly propped up if you can.

What's normal, and what's not

Expect for a few days:

  • Swelling, which is often most noticeable the morning after
  • Mild tenderness or a firm feeling
  • Small bruising at injection points

When to get in touch

Most reactions settle quickly. But you'll always have a point of contact, and you should get in touch promptly if you're worried about anything — severe or increasing pain, significant colour changes or anything that doesn't feel right. Being able to reach a qualified prescriber easily is one of the reasons nurse-led care matters.

Getting the best result

Stay hydrated, avoid pressing or massaging the lips unless advised, and be patient — lips can take up to a couple of weeks to fully settle, so judge the final result then, not on day one.

Common questions

How long does swelling last?

Most settles within two to three days, with lips fully settling over one to two weeks.

Can I wear lipstick afterwards?

Best to avoid makeup on the lips for the first 24 hours to keep the area clean.

When will I see the final result?

Give it up to two weeks once swelling has fully gone.
