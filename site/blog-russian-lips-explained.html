Russian lips: what the technique is and who it suits

Written by Eleana, Advanced Nurse Practitioner & independent prescriber

Russian lips is one of the most asked-about styles of lip fillers in Sheffield. It is a placement technique rather than a product, and it gives a particular kind of lifted, defined shape. Here is what it actually involves and who it tends to suit.

What the Russian lip technique is

The Russian lip technique is a way of placing hyaluronic acid dermal filler using small vertical microdroplets. Instead of filling along the body of the lip, the filler is worked upwards from the base of the lip towards the edge. The idea is to lift the lip and add a little height, creating a subtle heart shape with more definition through the centre and less forward projection.

In plain terms, it tends to make the lips look taller and more defined when viewed from the front, rather than fuller and more pushed-out from the side. That is the main reason people choose it.

How it differs from classic lip filler

Classic lip enhancement usually places filler more horizontally, adding volume and projection so the lips look plumper overall. The Russian technique focuses on vertical lift and shape. A few practical differences follow from that.

  • The Russian technique uses more injection points, because the filler is placed in many small amounts
  • It tends to emphasise height and definition over forward volume
  • It can look a little flatter from the side and more defined from the front

Neither approach is better than the other. They simply create different looks, which is exactly why an honest consultation matters before anything is placed.

Who it tends to suit

The Russian technique often appeals to people who want more definition and a gentle lift rather than obvious fullness, and to those who like the shape of a fuller cupid's bow. It can also suit people whose upper lip sits a little flat. As with any treatment, suitability depends on your natural lip shape, tissue and what you want, and it can only be judged properly at an assessment in person.

How long the results last

Russian lips use the same kind of hyaluronic acid dermal filler as other lip treatments, so longevity is broadly similar. For most people that means results in the region of six to twelve months before they gradually soften and the body breaks the filler down. The exact timing varies from person to person, so treat that as a guide rather than a promise.

Agreeing the look, and keeping it natural

The look is always agreed at your consultation before treatment begins. At A for Aesthetics the approach is conservative and natural, building shape carefully rather than overfilling in one sitting. A top-up can be added later if you would like a little more, which is far easier than trying to take volume away. Common questions

Are Russian lips more painful than normal lip filler?

The technique uses more injection points, so it can feel more involved. A numbing cream is applied first, and most people describe it as manageable rather than painful. Everyone experiences sensation differently.

Will Russian lips look obvious or overdone?

They do not have to. The aim is a lifted, defined shape that still looks like your own lips. The look is agreed at your consultation and placed conservatively, and a top-up can be added later if needed.

How much filler is used for Russian lips?

At A for Aesthetics the Russian lip technique starts from 1.1ml, with a 1.5ml option. The right amount for you is decided at your consultation and assessment with Eleana.

How long do Russian lips last?

As with other hyaluronic acid lip treatments, results tend to last somewhere in the region of six to twelve months before softening, though this varies from person to person.

Can Russian lips be dissolved?

Yes. Hyaluronic acid dermal filler can be dissolved if needed. This is one of the things you can discuss at your consultation.
