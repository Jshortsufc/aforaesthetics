What is Profhilo?

Profhilo is one of the most talked-about skin treatments of recent years, but it's often misunderstood. It isn't a dermal filler and it isn't an anti-wrinkle injection. Here's a clear, honest guide to what it actually does.

What is Profhilo, exactly?

Profhilo is an injectable skin booster made from stabilised hyaluronic acid — a substance your skin produces naturally to hold moisture. It's sometimes described as a "bio-remodelling" treatment because, rather than adding shape or volume, it spreads through the skin to hydrate it and support its underlying structure.

This is the key thing to understand: a Profhilo skin booster is designed to improve the quality and hydration of the skin itself. It's not filling a line or plumping a specific feature — it's working across a broader area to help the skin feel firmer, smoother and better hydrated.

Profhilo vs filler — and vs anti-wrinkle injections

Because they're all injectables, these treatments are easily confused. They do genuinely different jobs.

Profhilo vs filler

A dermal filler is placed in a specific area to add volume, structure or definition — think cheeks or lips. Profhilo isn't used that way. When people compare Profhilo vs filler, the honest answer is that they aren't really alternatives to each other: one shapes and volumises, the other conditions and hydrates the skin more broadly. Some people have both, for different reasons.

Profhilo vs anti-wrinkle injections

Anti-wrinkle injections work by temporarily relaxing specific muscles to soften movement lines. Profhilo doesn't touch the muscles at all — it works on the skin's hydration and texture. So they address different concerns and can be complementary rather than interchangeable.

What areas does Profhilo treat?

Profhilo is commonly used on the face, but it's also well suited to areas where skin quality and laxity become noticeable over time:

  • The face, for overall hydration and skin quality
  • The neck, where skin can become crêpey
  • The hands, which often show ageing early

What suits you depends on your skin, your goals and an in-person assessment — which is exactly what a consultation is for.

The typical treatment protocol

The standard manufacturer protocol for Profhilo is a course of two sessions, spaced around four weeks apart. This is publicly documented and widely used. Many people then have a maintenance session periodically, but your practitioner will talk you through what's appropriate for you rather than applying a fixed rule.

Each session is quick, and Profhilo is typically placed at a small number of points and allowed to spread through the skin.

What to expect

As with any injectable, you may notice small raised areas at the injection points that settle over a day or so, and some people have mild redness, tenderness or minor bruising. Results develop gradually as the skin responds, rather than appearing instantly — this is a treatment that rewards a little patience. Your practitioner will give you clear, personalised aftercare guidance.

We won't promise a specific outcome. How your skin responds is individual, which is why we assess it properly first.

Who does Profhilo suit?

Profhilo tends to appeal to people whose main concern is skin that feels dry, dull or a little lax, rather than a single line or a feature they want reshaped. It isn't right for everyone, and suitability — including your health and medical history — can only be judged at a consultation.

Common questions

Is Profhilo a dermal filler?

No. Profhilo is a hyaluronic acid skin booster designed to hydrate and remodel the skin across a wider area. It is not used to add shape or volume the way a dermal filler is.

How many Profhilo sessions do I need?

The standard manufacturer protocol is a course of two sessions spaced around four weeks apart. Whether that is right for you is discussed at consultation.

Is Profhilo the same as anti-wrinkle injections?

No. Anti-wrinkle injections work by relaxing specific muscles. Profhilo works on the quality and hydration of the skin itself, so they do different jobs.
