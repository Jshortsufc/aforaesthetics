Privacy Policy

This policy explains how A for Aesthetics collects and uses your personal information. This is a starting template — please have it reviewed to ensure it reflects your exact data practices and current UK GDPR / ICO requirements.

Who we are

A for Aesthetics Ltd (“we”, “us”) operates this website and provides aesthetic treatments in Sheffield and across South Yorkshire. For any privacy query, contact info@aforaesthetics.com.

What we collect

  • Contact details you provide (name, phone, email) when you enquire or book.
  • Health and medical information you provide as part of a consultation or consent process, where relevant to your care.
  • Basic technical data (such as anonymous analytics) when you use this website.

How we use it

  • To respond to enquiries and provide the treatments and aftercare you request.
  • To keep the clinical and consent records required of a healthcare provider.
  • To meet our legal and regulatory obligations.

Booking & third parties

Online booking and consent are handled by our booking provider (Faces / facesconsent.com), who process your information to deliver that service. We do not sell your personal information.

How long we keep it

Clinical records are retained for the periods required by professional and legal guidance. Other information is kept only as long as needed for the purpose it was collected.

Your rights

You have rights over your personal data, including access, correction and, in certain cases, erasure. To exercise them, contact us at info@aforaesthetics.com. You may also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO).
