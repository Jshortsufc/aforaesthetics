Dermal fillers for Chesterfield

Reviewed by Eleana, Advanced Nurse Practitioner & independent prescriber

Subtle, natural restoration of volume and contour for Chesterfield clients, carried out by a nurse prescriber at two welcoming Sheffield clinics. Every treatment begins with a free, no-obligation consultation.

The journey up from Chesterfield

Chesterfield lies about twelve miles south of Sheffield, and the A61 links the two towns almost the whole way, so it is a familiar and uncomplicated drive. Whether you are coming from the town centre or from Brimington, Whittington, Walton or Hasland, the road brings you straight up towards the city. Allowing for traffic, most clients reach us in around twenty-five to thirty-five minutes, and it is a route plenty of Chesterfield people already know well.

There are two clinics to choose from, so you can pick the one that fits your journey best:

  • And Collective, Abbeydale Road, in south Sheffield (S7), which is the closer of the two coming up from Chesterfield and sits right on the main road in as you enter the city. Parking is easy to find nearby.
  • Peaches, Hillsborough, in north-west Sheffield (S6), a good option if you are combining the visit with time in the city or heading on afterwards. There is parking close by and the tram runs nearby too.

Both addresses, with directions, are set out on our where we are page so you can work out your route in advance.

Why Chesterfield clients choose a nurse prescriber

Aesthetics is a fast-moving and largely unregulated field, and it is not always clear who is medically qualified and who is not. For many Chesterfield clients, that certainty is worth the short drive. Your treatment is carried out by Eleana, an Advanced Nurse Practitioner and independent prescriber with an MSc from Sheffield Hallam, eight years of experience and ACE accreditation. That means genuine clinical training, a detailed understanding of facial anatomy, and the ability to deal with the uncommon situations where a treatment needs reviewing.

It also means an honest opinion. If dermal filler is not the right answer, or if a more restrained plan would suit you better, Eleana will say so. There is no upselling and no pressure to make a decision on the day.

How dermal fillers work

Dermal fillers are a smooth hyaluronic acid gel, based on a substance the body produces naturally, used to restore volume and support and to refine the contour of the face. Placed carefully, they can rebuild fullness in the cheeks, bring definition to the chin and jaw, and soften deeper lines and folds. The point is to lift and freshen your features, not to alter them, and every result is subtle and assessed on an individual basis.

Your appointment opens with the consultation, where you and Eleana agree the plan and confirm the treatment suits you. The gel is then placed with precision and care, with symmetry and shape reviewed throughout so you stay in control of the outcome. You will leave with full aftercare guidance and someone to contact if you have questions later on.

A measured, individual result

Some people put off dermal fillers because the results they have noticed elsewhere look overdone. That is precisely what a careful, gradual approach avoids. Because the plan is agreed at the consultation and the filler is built up in stages, the result is shaped around your face rather than a fixed template. Many Chesterfield clients arrive wanting something understated, a little more support through the cheeks, a tidier jawline, or gentle softening of lines that have settled in over time, and each step remains entirely your choice.
