Anti-wrinkle injections for Doncaster

Reviewed by Eleana, Advanced Nurse Practitioner & independent prescriber

Natural-looking softening of frown, forehead and eye lines for Doncaster clients, assessed, prescribed and carried out by a nurse prescriber at two Sheffield clinics that are a clear run along the M18. Every treatment starts with a free consultation.

An easy trip from Doncaster

Doncaster is around twenty miles east of Sheffield, and thanks to the motorway it is a good deal quicker to reach than the distance suggests. The M18 links up with the A630 to bring you almost door to door, and whether you are setting off from Bessacarr, Bawtry or Armthorpe the roads are simple to follow. Most clients find the drive takes somewhere between thirty and forty minutes depending on the time of day.

We treat from two locations, so you can pick whichever fits your journey in from Doncaster:

  • Peaches, Hillsborough (S6), north-west Sheffield, handy if your route brings you across the top of the city. Easy parking nearby and the tram stops close by.

Why Doncaster clients choose a nurse prescriber

Anti-wrinkle injections use a prescription-only medicine, which by law must be prescribed by a suitably qualified prescriber after a face-to-face assessment, rather than sold like a beauty product. That is the main reason clients are content to make the trip in from Doncaster. Your treatment is assessed, prescribed and carried out by Eleana, an Advanced Nurse Practitioner and independent prescriber with an MSc from Sheffield Hallam, eight years of experience and ACE accreditation, so you are in genuinely clinical hands throughout.

It also means honesty. If a treatment is not right for you, or a lighter touch would serve you better, you will be told plainly. Nobody is upsold, and there is never any pressure to book on the day.

What the treatment involves

Anti-wrinkle injections relax the specific muscles behind certain expression lines, so the lines above them soften over the following week or two. The areas people ask about most are the frown lines between the brows, lines across the forehead and the crow's feet at the corners of the eyes. Whatever the area, the aim at A for Aesthetics is a fresher, more relaxed look, never frozen.

Your appointment starts with a consultation and assessment, where Eleana confirms the treatment is suitable and agrees a conservative plan with you. Treatment itself is quick, and you leave with full aftercare advice and a review to check how things have settled.

Natural-looking results, agreed with you first

Some people hold off on anti-wrinkle treatment because they have seen results that look overdone. That is not how it works here. Because everything is agreed at the consultation and kept deliberately subtle, the result is shaped around your face and your expressions rather than a fixed formula. Many Doncaster clients are not after anything dramatic, just to soften the lines that have started to settle in, while still looking like themselves.

Why there are no before-and-after photos or brand names

Anti-wrinkle injections are among the most popular treatments we do, so Doncaster clients often ask why there is no before-and-after gallery here and no product brand names. It is the law, not the results. The medicine used is a prescription-only medicine, and in the UK it is illegal to advertise a prescription-only medicine to the public. That rule is set out in the Human Medicines Regulations 2012, overseen by the MHRA, with advertising standards also applied by the ASA. It means no clinic may name the brand, show before-and-after photos of the treatment, or promote it with offers. If you see a clinic doing so, the rules are being broken, and we would rather stay on the right side of them. What we can do instead is give you honest, realistic expectations face to face at your free consultation.

Common questions from Doncaster clients

How far is it from Doncaster?

Doncaster is around twenty miles east of Sheffield, an easy run along the M18 and then the A630. Most clients reach either clinic in around thirty to forty minutes depending on traffic and where in Doncaster you set off from. Parking is straightforward at both.

How long do results last?

Typically around three to four months before gradually softening. It varies from person to person, and Eleana will give you a realistic idea at your consultation.

Will I still be able to make expressions?

The aim is a natural, refreshed look rather than a frozen one. A conservative approach keeps movement while softening the lines that bother you, all agreed with you first.

Is there a consultation first?

Yes. It is a prescription-only treatment, so a free, face-to-face assessment with a nurse prescriber is required to confirm suitability before anything is arranged.
