# Image checklist — A for Aesthetics

Every image slot in the site is a labelled placeholder (search the HTML for `hero-media--placeholder`, or the `<figure>` blocks on treatment pages). Drop a file into `/img/` with the filename shown, then replace the placeholder `<div>` with an `<img src="/img/FILENAME" alt="...">`.

## Read this first (important for a clinic)

- **Your own photos are best.** Real clinic, real practitioner, and real before/afters (with written client consent) beat any stock image — and they boost your Google trust ranking. Use these wherever you can.
- **Don't use random images off Google.** Most are copyrighted, and any photo of a face implies that person is your client and endorses treatment (a consent issue). Only use images you own or that are licensed for commercial use.
- **Free, commercially-licensed sources:** Unsplash, Pexels, Pixabay. **Paid (higher quality):** Adobe Stock, iStock.
- Save as JPG or WebP, compressed. Landscape shots ~1600px wide; portraits ~1200px wide.

## Palette to match
Pick images with soft, warm, coral/blush/neutral tones so they sit with the brand (`#e08e83`). Avoid cold blue-toned or heavily filtered stock — it'll clash.
