Non-surgical nose reshaping (liquid rhinoplasty): what to know

Written by Eleana, Advanced Nurse Practitioner & independent prescriber

Non-surgical nose reshaping, sometimes called liquid rhinoplasty, uses hyaluronic acid dermal filler to refine the look of the nose without surgery. It is one of our advanced treatments in Sheffield, and because the nose is a sensitive area, it deserves a careful, honest explanation.

What it can do

By placing small amounts of hyaluronic acid dermal filler in precise positions, this treatment can make subtle improvements to the profile and balance of the nose. It is the same family of product used in our wider dermal filler work, placed with a very different purpose. In practice it may be able to:

  • Smooth the appearance of a small bump on the bridge
  • Lift or refine a drooping or rounded tip
  • Improve the overall balance and straightness of the profile from the side

It works by adding volume in the right places so the eye reads a smoother, straighter line, not by removing anything.

What it cannot do

It is just as important to be clear about the limits. Non-surgical nose reshaping cannot make the nose smaller, because it adds filler rather than removing tissue. It cannot change the underlying bone, and it cannot correct a deviated septum or improve breathing. If any of those are your goal, the right step is a medical or surgical opinion, not filler. An honest practitioner will tell you when a treatment is not the answer.

Who it tends to suit

It often suits people who are bothered by a small bump, a slightly drooping tip or a profile they feel is a little uneven, and who want a temporary, non-surgical change. Suitability always depends on your individual anatomy and what you want to achieve, which can only be judged at an assessment in person.

Why safety matters so much here

The nose sits close to important blood vessels, which makes it one of the higher-risk areas to treat with filler. For that reason it should only ever be carried out by an experienced, medically qualified, prescriber-led injector who understands the anatomy and can recognise and manage a complication if one arises. At A for Aesthetics the treatment is carried out by Eleana, a registered nurse and independent prescriber, after a full assessment. This is not a treatment to choose on price alone.

Results and downtime

Results are temporary and tend to last somewhere in the region of nine to eighteen months before the body gradually breaks the filler down, though this varies from person to person. Downtime is usually minimal, with the possibility of some mild swelling, tenderness or small bruising that settles over a few days. Common questions

Does non-surgical nose reshaping make the nose smaller?

No. It adds hyaluronic acid dermal filler to smooth or rebalance the profile, so it cannot remove tissue or make the nose smaller overall. It can, however, make a bump look smoother and the profile look more balanced.

Is non-surgical nose reshaping safe?

The nose is a higher-risk area because of the blood vessels around it, so it should only be treated by an experienced, medically qualified, prescriber-led injector who can recognise and manage a complication. At A for Aesthetics it is carried out by Eleana, a registered nurse and independent prescriber.

How long do the results last?

Results are temporary. They usually last somewhere in the region of nine to eighteen months before the body gradually breaks the filler down, though this varies from person to person.

Is there any downtime?

Downtime is usually minimal. You may have some mild swelling, tenderness or small bruising that settles over a few days. You will be given aftercare advice to follow.

Can it fix breathing problems or a deviated septum?

No. This is a cosmetic treatment for the appearance of the nose only. It cannot change bone, straighten a deviated septum or improve breathing, which are matters for a medical or surgical opinion.
