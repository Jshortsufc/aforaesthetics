Anti-Wrinkle Injections in Sheffield

A consultation-led treatment to gently soften expression lines — for a fresher, more rested look that still moves naturally.

What it helps with

Anti-wrinkle injections are used to relax the muscles that create certain expression lines, softening their appearance over time. It's a popular, non-surgical treatment when carried out carefully and with realistic expectations. At A for Aesthetics it's always led by a proper consultation, so it's only recommended where it's genuinely suitable for you.

Areas often treated

  • Frown lines between the brows
  • Forehead lines
  • Crow's feet around the eyes

Suitable areas and whether the treatment is right for you are confirmed at your consultation.

What happens at your appointment

  • Consultation and assessment. We discuss your goals and check suitability.
  • Treatment. If appropriate, the treatment is carried out precisely and comfortably.
  • Aftercare. You'll get clear advice and know exactly what to expect next.

Prescribed and administered by a nurse prescriber

Anti-wrinkle injections are a prescription-only treatment — which, by law, means they must be prescribed by a suitable qualified prescriber after a face-to-face assessment. At A for Aesthetics that's Eleana, an Advanced Nurse Practitioner and independent prescriber with a master's in advanced clinical practice and eight years' experience. So the person assessing you, prescribing and carrying out your treatment is one and the same clinician — not a beauty therapist working to someone else's prescription.

What to expect afterwards

  • Results develop gradually, typically over one to two weeks.
  • Follow the aftercare advice given, including staying upright and avoiding rubbing the area for the rest of the day.
  • A review can be arranged so we can check you're happy with how it's settled.
Anti-wrinkle treatments are prescription-only and provided only where clinically appropriate, following a consultation and assessment. This page is general information and not an advertisement for any specific medicine.

Common questions

What areas can it treat?

Common areas include frown lines between the brows, forehead lines and crow's feet around the eyes. Your practitioner will assess what's suitable for you at your consultation.

When will I see results?

Results tend to develop gradually over one to two weeks. Your practitioner will explain what to expect and when to check back in.

Is a consultation required first?

Yes — this is a consultation-led treatment. A free assessment is carried out first to confirm suitability before anything is arranged.
