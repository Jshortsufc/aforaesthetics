Anti-wrinkle injections · Sheffield

Anti-Wrinkle Injections in Sheffield

Reviewed by Eleana, Advanced Nurse Practitioner & independent prescriber

Smooth, natural-looking softening of expression lines, carried out by a nurse prescriber who assesses, prescribes and treats you personally. Every treatment starts with a free, no-obligation consultation.

What anti-wrinkle injections do

Anti-wrinkle injections relax the specific muscles that create certain expression lines, so the lines above them gradually soften. Done carefully and conservatively, the result is a fresher, more relaxed look, not a frozen one. The aim here is always subtlety: keeping natural movement while taking the edge off the lines that bother you.

Areas we treat

  • Frown lines between the brows
  • Forehead lines
  • Crow's feet around the eyes
  • Brow lift and bunny lines
  • Gummy smile and fine lip lines
  • Jaw slimming / teeth grinding (clenching)
  • Neck smoothing
  • Excessive sweating (hyperhidrosis)

Which areas are suitable for you is confirmed at your face-to-face consultation and assessment, never assumed in advance.

Who they suit

  • Softening frown, forehead or eye lines that have started to settle in
  • A preventative approach to lines that deepen with expression
  • Managing teeth grinding or a heavy jaw
  • Excessive underarm sweating

Suitability is always assessed individually. If a treatment isn't right for you, we'll say so.

What happens at your appointment

  • Consultation & assessment. We talk through what's bothering you and Eleana assesses whether treatment is suitable.
  • Prescribing. Because it's a prescription-only medicine, the same nurse prescriber who assesses you also prescribes it, after that assessment, never before.
  • Treatment. A series of tiny injections is placed precisely. It's quick and most people find it very manageable.
  • Review. Results build over one to two weeks, and a review can be arranged to check how it has settled.

Why a nurse prescriber matters

Anti-wrinkle injections use a prescription-only medicine, which by law must be prescribed by a suitably qualified prescriber after a face-to-face assessment. At A for Aesthetics that's Eleana, an Advanced Nurse Practitioner and independent prescriber with an MSc in advanced clinical practice and eight years' experience. So the person assessing you, prescribing and carrying out your treatment is one and the same clinician, working to a medical standard, not a therapist administering someone else's prescription.

Why you won't see before-and-after photos here

Anti-wrinkle injections are one of the most popular treatments we do, so it's a fair question why there's no before-and-after gallery on this page and no product brand names. The reason is the law, not a lack of results.

The medicine used is a prescription-only medicine. In the UK it is illegal to advertise a prescription-only medicine to the public. That rule is set out in the Human Medicines Regulations 2012 and overseen by the MHRA, with advertising standards also applied by the ASA. In plain terms, no clinic is allowed to name the brand of product, show before-and-after photos of the treatment, or promote it with special offers to the public.

So if you see another clinic naming brands or posting anti-wrinkle before-and-afters, that isn't something we're holding back. Those rules are being broken. We'd rather stay on the right side of them, and it's the same standard that means your treatment is assessed, prescribed and carried out in person by a nurse prescriber.

What we can do, and would much rather do, is talk you through honest, realistic expectations face to face at your free consultation, with no pressure and no photos dressed up as promises.

Aftercare

  • Stay upright and avoid rubbing or massaging the area for the rest of the day.
  • Avoid heavy exercise, alcohol and heat for the first 24 hours.
  • Results develop gradually, give it up to two weeks before judging.
  • Get in touch any time if you have questions after your treatment.

Common questions

What areas can it treat?

Common areas include frown lines, forehead lines and crow's feet, plus a brow lift, gummy smile, lip lines, jaw slimming, neck and excessive sweating. Suitability is assessed individually at your consultation.

When will I see results?

Results tend to develop gradually over one to two weeks.

How long do results last?

Typically around three to four months before gradually softening. It varies from person to person, and Eleana will give you a realistic idea at your consultation.

Is a consultation required first?

Yes. It's a prescription-only treatment, so a free, face-to-face assessment with a nurse prescriber is required to confirm suitability before anything is arranged.
