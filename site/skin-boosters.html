Skin Boosters in Sheffield

Reviewed by Eleana, Advanced Nurse Practitioner & independent prescriber

Injectable hydration for smoother, fresher, better-quality skin — a subtle glow-from-within rather than added volume, delivered by a nurse prescriber.

What are skin boosters?

Skin boosters are injectable treatments that improve the skin's hydration, smoothness and overall quality from within, rather than adding volume or changing your shape. They're a popular choice for a healthy, refreshed glow across the face, neck or hands.

Who they suit

They're well suited to:

  • Dull, dry or tired-looking skin
  • Fine 'crepey' texture
  • Anyone wanting a natural glow without added volume
  • Skin health as part of a wider plan

What to expect

After a consultation to confirm suitability, treatment is quick and comfortable. Skin boosters often work best as a short initial course, with results building over a few weeks. Eleana will set out a realistic plan at your consultation.

Aftercare

Minor bumps or redness at the injection points are normal and settle within a day or two. You'll be given simple aftercare to follow.

Common questions

How soon will I see results?

Skin quality improves gradually over a few weeks, and is often best after a short course.

How long does it last?

Typically several months, after which a top-up maintains the effect. We'll advise at your consultation.

Is it the same as filler?

No — skin boosters improve skin quality and hydration rather than adding volume or contour like dermal fillers.
