Fat Dissolving in Sheffield

A non-surgical option to target small, stubborn pockets of fat that don't shift with diet and exercise — assessed and carried out by a nurse prescriber.

What is fat dissolving?

Fat dissolving injections target specific, small areas of stubborn fat — such as under the chin or other defined pockets — to help improve contour. It's not a weight-loss treatment or a substitute for a healthy lifestyle; it's for those last stubborn areas that stay put despite your best efforts.

Who it suits

It's best suited to:

  • Small, defined pockets of fat
  • Areas that resist diet and exercise
  • People close to their target shape wanting refinement
  • Those looking for a non-surgical option

What happens

Suitability is assessed carefully at your free consultation — this treatment isn't right for everyone, and we'll tell you honestly if it isn't right for you. Where suitable, treatment is carried out in the area agreed, often over a short course of sessions for the best result.

Aftercare

Swelling, tenderness and firmness in the treated area are expected for a while afterwards as the area settles — this is part of how it works. You'll be given full aftercare guidance and a point of contact.

Common questions

How many sessions will I need?

Often a short course, spaced a few weeks apart, depending on the area. We'll set out a realistic plan at consultation.

Is it permanent?

Once treated fat is cleared it doesn't return, but maintaining your result still relies on a stable, healthy lifestyle.

Is it suitable for larger areas?

No — it's designed for small, stubborn pockets, not weight loss. We'll be honest about whether it's right for you.
