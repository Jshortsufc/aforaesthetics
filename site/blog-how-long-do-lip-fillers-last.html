How long do lip fillers last?

Written by Eleana, Advanced Nurse Practitioner & independent prescriber

It's the most common question we're asked about lip fillers — and the honest answer is “it depends.” Here's what actually affects how long your results last.

The short answer

For most people, lip filler lasts somewhere in the region of six to twelve months before it gradually softens and the body naturally breaks it down. That's a broad range because longevity genuinely varies from person to person — there's no single number that's true for everyone.

What affects how long it lasts

Several things influence longevity:

  • Your metabolism — faster metabolisms tend to break filler down quicker
  • How much was placed and the product used
  • The area and how much it moves
  • Lifestyle factors like sun exposure and general health

How to keep your results looking their best

Stay hydrated, protect your lips from strong sun, follow your aftercare, and book a review if you'd like to top up before it fully softens. Chasing “more” isn't the goal — a subtle top-up when needed keeps things looking natural rather than overfilled.

A note on doing it well

Longevity matters, but who treats you matters more. A qualified practitioner will treat conservatively, keep your results balanced, and be honest about when you don't need more — which is exactly the approach at A for Aesthetics.

Common questions

How soon can I top up?

Usually once results begin to soften — your practitioner will advise at a review. Topping up too often isn't recommended.

Do lip fillers stretch your lips?

Treated conservatively by a skilled practitioner, no. That's why an unhurried, natural approach matters.

Can lip filler be dissolved?

Yes, hyaluronic acid fillers can be dissolved if needed. This is discussed at your consultation.
