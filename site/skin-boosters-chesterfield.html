What skin boosters are, and what they are not

Skin boosters are injectable treatments that work on the quality of the skin itself, improving hydration, smoothness and that healthy, lit-from-within look. This is where they differ from a dermal filler: rather than adding volume or reshaping the face, they aim to make your existing skin look fresher and more supple. They can be used on the face and also on areas such as the neck and the backs of the hands, which often show tiredness and crepiness earlier.

Depending on your skin, this may involve treatments such as polynucleotides or Profhilo. Eleana will talk through which option makes sense for you, and how many sessions to plan for, during your consultation rather than deciding it in advance.

Signs skin boosters might help

Chesterfield clients often ask about skin boosters when they notice:

  • Skin that looks dull, dry or a little tired even after resting
  • Fine, crepey texture on the cheeks, around the eyes, the neck or the hands
  • A wish to look refreshed and glowing without any change to their features or volume
  • An interest in maintaining skin quality steadily over time rather than a single quick change

Your appointment, step by step

Everything begins with the consultation, where suitability is confirmed and a plan agreed. The treatment is quick and usually well tolerated, delivered through a number of small injection points. A few tiny bumps or some mild redness afterwards are normal and typically settle within a day or two. You will leave with clear aftercare guidance and are welcome to get in touch afterwards with any questions.

Common questions from Chesterfield clients

How long does it take to get to you from Chesterfield?

Chesterfield is around twelve miles south of Sheffield, and the A61 runs almost the whole way. From Brimington, Whittington, Walton or Hasland most clients reach one of our clinics in roughly twenty-five to thirty-five minutes, depending on traffic and time of day.

What is the difference between skin boosters and filler?

Skin boosters improve hydration, glow and skin quality from within, so the change is in how healthy and fresh the skin looks. A dermal filler is a different treatment used to add volume or contour.

Will I need more than one session?

Skin boosters usually give the best result as a short initial course, with the effect building over a few weeks. Eleana will recommend a realistic plan at your free consultation.

Do you assess suitability first?

Yes, every treatment starts with a free, no-obligation consultation so we can check the treatment is right for you and agree what you want to achieve.
