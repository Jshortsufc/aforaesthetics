Anti-wrinkle injections aftercare: the do's and don'ts

Anti-wrinkle injections are quick and straightforward, but what you do in the hours and days afterwards helps you get the best, most even result. Here's honest, nurse-led guidance on the do's and don'ts — and what's completely normal along the way.

What's normal straight after treatment

Anti-wrinkle injections use botulinum toxin, a prescription-only medicine, placed into small muscles to soften the movement that creates lines. It's a fast treatment with very little downtime, so most people head straight back to their day.

In the first hour or so, it's common to see small raised bumps at the injection points — a little like nettle rash. These usually flatten within about 15 to 30 minutes. You might also notice slight redness, mild tenderness, or the odd pinpoint of bruising. None of this is unusual, and it typically settles quickly. You won't see any effect on your lines yet — that's expected, because the result builds gradually rather than appearing on the day.

The key do's

A few simple habits give the product the best chance to settle exactly where Eleana placed it:

  • Stay upright for around four hours. Keeping your head up rather than lying flat helps the product stay in the treated muscles.
  • Keep gently active. Light, normal movement through your face — talking, smiling, raising your eyebrows in the first hour if advised — is fine. Just keep it relaxed and natural.
  • Keep the area clean. If you need to, you can gently apply makeup later that day, but the lighter the touch the better.
  • Follow your practitioner's specific advice. The guidance you're given at your appointment is tailored to you and always takes priority over anything general you read online, including this article.

The don'ts — what not to do after anti-wrinkle injections

Just as useful is knowing what to avoid while everything settles. These are the main things not to do after anti-wrinkle injections:

  • Don't rub or massage the area. Avoid pressing, rubbing or massaging the treated areas for the rest of the day, as this can move the product away from where it was intended.
  • Don't do strenuous exercise or expose yourself to heat for about 24 to 48 hours. That means holding off on the gym, running, saunas, steam rooms, sunbeds and very hot baths for a day or two.
  • Don't lie down flat straight away. Give it those first few hours upright before reclining fully or going to bed.
  • Don't book facials or skin treatments over the area for about two weeks. Facials, microneedling, massages and similar treatments across the treated zone are best left until the result has fully settled.
  • Go easy on alcohol. It's sensible to keep alcohol to a minimum for the first day, as it can make any bruising more noticeable.

None of these are about anything going wrong — they simply give the treatment the calm, undisturbed conditions it needs to work evenly.

When your results appear

Anti-wrinkle injections don't work instantly, and that's completely normal. You'll usually start to notice a gentle softening of movement over the first few days. The effect then continues to build, with the full result generally visible at around two weeks.

Because of this, it's best not to judge your outcome too early. Give it the full two weeks before deciding how you feel about it. If you'd like any fine-tuning, that two-week point is also when a review makes sense — many practitioners offer a check-in around then. You can read more about how the treatment itself works on our anti-wrinkle injections page.

When to contact your practitioner

The vast majority of people have a smooth, uneventful recovery. Even so, you should always have a clear point of contact — and being able to reach a qualified prescriber easily is one of the real advantages of nurse-led care.

Get in touch with your practitioner if you notice anything that doesn't feel right, such as increasing or severe pain, signs of infection like spreading redness, swelling or heat at an injection site, drooping of an eyelid or brow, difficulty with normal facial movement, or any reaction that concerns you. Reaching out early is always the right thing to do — no question is too small. Common questions

How long until anti-wrinkle injections start working?

You'll usually start to notice a softening over the first few days, with the full effect building over about two weeks. It's best to judge your result at that two-week point rather than in the first day or two.

When can I exercise after anti-wrinkle injections?

Keep to gentle activity on the day and avoid strenuous exercise, saunas, steam rooms and other heat for roughly 24 to 48 hours. Eleana will confirm the right timing for you at your appointment.

Can I lie down or have a facial after treatment?

Try to stay upright for around four hours rather than lying flat straight away, and avoid facials, massages or other treatments over the area for about two weeks so the product settles where it was placed.

Is it normal to have small bumps or redness afterwards?

Small raised bumps, minor redness or slight tenderness at the injection points are common in the first hour or so and usually settle quickly. Occasional pinpoint bruising can also occur. If anything worries you, contact your practitioner.
