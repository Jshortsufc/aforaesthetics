Your first consultation: what to expect

If you've never had a treatment before, the consultation can feel like the nerve-wracking part. It shouldn't be — it's the most important and most reassuring step. Here's what happens.

It's a conversation, not a sales pitch

A good consultation starts with listening. Eleana will ask what's bothering you or what you'd like to achieve, and talk through the realistic options — including whether a treatment is right for you at all. There's never any pressure to go ahead.

A proper assessment

Because treatments should be tailored, your consultation includes a genuine assessment of your face, skin and suitability, plus a chat about your medical history. For prescription-only treatments this assessment is a legal requirement, not a formality — and it's carried out by a qualified prescriber.

Honest, tailored advice

You'll get an honest recommendation: what would help, what wouldn't, what it involves, realistic results and aftercare. Sometimes the best advice is to do less — or nothing — and a trustworthy practitioner will tell you that.

No pressure to decide

You can take your time. Many people consult, go away and think, and book in when they're ready. That's exactly how it should be.

Common questions

Is the consultation really free?

Yes — a free, no-obligation consultation is offered to every client.

Will I be treated on the same day?

Sometimes, if it's suitable and you're ready — but there's never any pressure to decide on the spot.

What should I bring?

Just yourself and any questions. It helps to know your medical history and any medications you take.

This article is general information, not medical advice. Whether a treatment is suitable for you can only be decided at a personal consultation and assessment.
