The 8-point facelift: a non-surgical liquid facelift explained

Written by Eleana, Advanced Nurse Practitioner & independent prescriber

The 8-point facelift is a non-surgical, or liquid, facelift that uses structured dermal filler to lift and restore volume across the face. It is one of our advanced treatments in Sheffield, and the name refers to the key points where filler is placed rather than to any surgery.

What the 8-point facelift is

As we age, the face gradually loses volume and the support structures beneath the skin change. That can make the cheeks look flatter, soften the jawline and make the whole face look a little more tired. The 8-point facelift addresses this by placing hyaluronic acid dermal filler at several carefully chosen points across the cheeks, mid-face, jawline and around the mouth.

It uses the same family of product found in our wider dermal filler treatments, but the thinking is different. Rather than treating one feature, the aim is to restore support where volume has been lost, so the face looks lifted and refreshed as a whole. Treating these points together often gives a more balanced result than treating any one area in isolation.

How it is planned

Because it works across the whole face, the treatment is assessed and planned before anything is placed. At your consultation Eleana will look at how your face has changed, where volume has been lost and what you would like, then agree a plan with you. The approach is conservative and natural, building subtle structure rather than overfilling any single point.

What it can and cannot do

A liquid facelift is well suited to volume loss. It can restore fullness to flattened cheeks, soften the shadows that volume loss creates and give the face a more lifted, rested appearance. What it is not is a replacement for a surgical facelift. It does not remove or tighten loose skin, so if your main concern is significant sagging rather than lost volume, an honest consultation may point you towards a different option. Knowing which problem you are actually solving is the most important part.

Who it tends to suit

It often suits people in their thirties onwards who notice their face looks flatter or more tired than it used to, and who want a subtle, non-surgical refresh with no downtime for a hospital procedure. Suitability is always judged individually at an assessment. You can see current pricing on our prices page, where the 8-point facelift is £350.

Results and downtime

Results are temporary and commonly last somewhere in the region of twelve to eighteen months before the body gradually breaks the filler down, though this varies from person to person. Downtime is usually minimal, with the possibility of some swelling, tenderness or small bruising that settles over a few days.

Common questions

Is the 8-point facelift the same as a surgical facelift?

No. It is a non-surgical treatment using hyaluronic acid dermal filler to restore volume and lift at key points. It is not a replacement for a surgical facelift and it does not remove or tighten loose skin.

Who does the 8-point facelift suit?

It tends to suit people whose main concern is volume loss and a flatter, more tired-looking face, rather than significant loose or sagging skin. Suitability is assessed individually at your consultation.

Will I look obviously done?

The aim is a subtle, natural refresh. The treatment is assessed and planned first, and filler is placed conservatively across several points to restore balance rather than overfill any one area.

How long do the results last?

Results are temporary. They commonly last somewhere in the region of twelve to eighteen months before the body gradually breaks the filler down, though this varies from person to person.

Is there much downtime?

Downtime is usually minimal. You may have some swelling, tenderness or small bruising that settles over a few days. You will be given aftercare advice to follow afterwards.
