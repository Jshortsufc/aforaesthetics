Our approach

Clinical care, without the conveyor belt.

A for Aesthetics is a small, personal clinic — deliberately so. There's no hard sell and no one-size-fits-all. Every treatment begins with a proper consultation, and only goes ahead if it's genuinely right for you. Because Eleana is a nurse prescriber, the same clinician assesses you, prescribes where needed, treats you and looks after your aftercare — start to finish.

Safety first, always

The non-surgical aesthetics sector is moving toward tighter regulation across the UK, with new licensing standards for premises and practitioners. Choosing a qualified nurse prescriber means you're already in hands that work to a medical standard — proper assessment, clinical record-keeping, honest advice about suitability, and the knowledge to manage the rare occasions something needs attention.

Professional registrations: Eleana is an NMC-registered nurse and independent prescriber. Add any additional memberships here (for example Save Face, JCCP or BACN) once confirmed, as these are strong trust signals.
