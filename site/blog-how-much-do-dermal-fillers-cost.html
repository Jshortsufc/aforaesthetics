How much do dermal fillers cost in Sheffield?

Written by Eleana, Advanced Nurse Practitioner & independent prescriber

Dermal filler covers a lot of different treatments, from cheeks and chin to jawline and smile lines, so there is no single price. Here is an honest look at what you can expect to pay in the UK and here in Sheffield, what actually drives the cost, and why the cheapest option is rarely the safe one.

What affects the price of dermal fillers?

Unlike a single set fee, dermal filler pricing reflects several things. Understanding them helps you compare like for like rather than just chasing the lowest number.

  • The area treated. Cheeks, chin, jawline, tear troughs, smile lines and non-surgical nose reshaping each need a different approach. Some areas take more product and more precision than others, so they are priced separately.
  • Amount of product (ml). Filler is measured in millilitres. A single area using around 1ml costs less than a combined treatment such as jaw and chin together. More product means more cost, but more is not always better, the right amount depends on your face and your goals.
  • Product quality. Reputable clinics use well-established, regulated hyaluronic acid dermal fillers. Quality products cost more to buy in, and that is reflected in the price.
  • Practitioner expertise. A treatment carried out by an experienced medical professional who can assess your face, inject safely and manage any complication is not the same service as a quick appointment with an untrained injector, even if the result looks similar on paper.
  • Location and clinic. Prices tend to be higher in central London than in much of the North. Sheffield and South Yorkshire sit comfortably within the typical UK range rather than at the top of it.

Typical UK dermal filler prices

As a general guide, dermal filler in the UK usually costs somewhere from around £150 to £350 or more per area, with larger or combined treatments sitting higher. These are broad figures, not a quote, the only way to know your price is a proper consultation and assessment.

At A for Aesthetics, dermal filler is priced by area so you only pay for what you need. For example, marionette (mouth-to-chin) lines start from £160, chin filler is £165, tear trough is £195, jaw contouring is £200, and cheek enhancement and smile lines are £220. Combined and larger treatments, such as a jaw and chin package or a full-face approach, are priced higher. You can see current pricing in full on our prices page, and read more about the treatments themselves on our dermal fillers page.

Why very cheap dermal filler is a red flag

It is tempting to book the lowest price you can find, but very cheap dermal filler is one of the clearest warning signs in this industry. A price well below the typical range often means one or more corners have been cut: a less experienced injector, an unknown or unbranded product, or no real consultation, medical history check or aftercare.

Dermal filler is a medical procedure. Complications are uncommon, but when they happen, for example a blocked blood vessel, they need to be recognised and treated quickly by someone qualified to prescribe. At A for Aesthetics your treatment is carried out by Eleana, an Advanced Nurse Practitioner and independent prescriber, which means the person treating you is also the person able to manage a complication safely. That clinical safety net is a large part of what you are paying for, and it is not something a bargain price can offer.

What's included in the price?

A fair price should cover more than the few minutes of injecting. At A for Aesthetics it includes:

  • A free, no-obligation consultation before any treatment, so you are assessed properly and given honest advice, including being told if filler is not right for you.
  • The treatment itself, using a quality regulated hyaluronic acid dermal filler.
  • Clear aftercare guidance and a point of contact if you have any concerns afterwards.

Every treatment starts with that free consultation for a reason: it lets us understand what you want, check it is suitable and set realistic expectations before you commit to anything.

Getting a price that's right for you

The honest answer to "how much do dermal fillers cost?" is: it depends on the area, the amount of product and who you trust to do the work. Rather than booking on price alone, look for a qualified, prescribing practitioner, a clear consultation and a price that sits sensibly within the UK range. If you would like a personalised idea of cost, the best next step is a free consultation, you can get in touch to arrange one.
