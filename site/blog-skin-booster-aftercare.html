Skin booster aftercare: what to do and what to avoid

Written by Eleana, Advanced Nurse Practitioner & independent prescriber

Good aftercare helps your skin settle comfortably and gives your treatment the best chance to work. Here is a calm, practical guide to what to expect in the first day or two after skin boosters, what to avoid for a short while, and when to expect results. This applies to skin boosters generally, including Profhilo-type treatments and polynucleotides.

What is normal in the first day or two

Skin boosters are injected across the treated area, so it is normal to see small raised bumps at the injection points straight afterwards. These are simply the product sitting under the skin, and they usually settle within a day or two as it spreads and is absorbed. Mild redness, slight tenderness or the odd small bruise can also happen and tends to ease quickly. None of this is a cause for concern, and it is a normal part of how the treatment beds in.

If anything feels unusual, or you are worried at any point, get in touch. Being a point of contact after treatment is part of nurse-led care.

What to do

  • Keep the area clean. Leave the treated skin alone and clean for the rest of the day, so the tiny injection points can close without anything being pressed into them.
  • Stay hydrated. Skin boosters work with your skin's own hydration, so drinking plenty of water supports the process and helps you feel comfortable.
  • Be gentle. Cleanse lightly and avoid rubbing, massaging or picking at the area while it settles.
  • Protect from the sun. Use sun protection on the treated area and avoid direct sun and sunbeds for a few days while the skin recovers.
  • Follow your specific aftercare. You will be given clear guidance to suit your treatment, so follow that first if anything differs from these general points.

What to avoid, and for how long

For a short period after treatment, usually around 24 to 48 hours, it helps to avoid anything that raises heat or blood flow to the area, or that risks introducing bacteria to the injection points. As a general guide:

  • Makeup on the treated area, ideally until the following morning, to keep the injection points clean.
  • Heat such as saunas, steam rooms, hot baths and very hot showers.
  • Strenuous exercise and anything that gets you hot and flushed.
  • Alcohol for the first day or so, as it can add to redness and swelling.
  • Other facial treatments such as facials, peels or saunas at the gym until the skin has fully settled.

These are sensible general timings. Always follow the specific aftercare Eleana gives you, as it is tailored to your treatment and your skin.

When results appear

Skin boosters are not an instant fix, and that is by design. Rather than adding volume, they improve hydration, smoothness and overall skin quality, and that builds gradually over the following weeks. Because of this, a short initial course is often advised to get the best from the treatment, with results maintained by occasional top-ups after that. Common questions

Are small bumps after skin boosters normal?

Yes. Small raised bumps at the injection points are common with skin boosters and usually settle within a day or two as the product spreads and is absorbed. Mild redness or tenderness can also be expected and eases quickly.

When can I wear makeup after skin boosters?

It is best to leave makeup off the treated area for the rest of the day, usually until the following morning, to keep the tiny injection points clean while they close. Your practitioner will confirm the right timing for you.

When will I see results from skin boosters?

Skin boosters improve hydration and skin quality gradually over the following weeks rather than straight away, and results are often best after a short initial course. Eleana will set out a realistic plan at your consultation.

How long should I avoid exercise, heat and alcohol?

As a general guide, avoid strenuous exercise, saunas, steam rooms, other heat and alcohol for around 24 to 48 hours. Follow the specific aftercare you are given, and get in touch if you have any questions.
