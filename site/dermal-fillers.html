Dermal fillers · Sheffield

Dermal Fillers in Sheffield

Reviewed by Eleana, Advanced Nurse Practitioner & independent prescriber

Subtle restoration of volume, structure and definition — cheeks, chin, jawline and softening of deeper lines — tailored to your face and always led by a free consultation with Eleana.

What are dermal fillers?

Dermal fillers use a premium gel to gently restore volume and support where the face has lost it over time, or to add definition and balance. Used carefully, they refresh and lift rather than change how you look — the aim is a natural, well-rested result, never overfilled.

Areas we treat

Every face is different, so what's right for you is agreed at your consultation. Commonly treated areas include:

  • Cheeks — to restore lost volume and lift
  • Chin and jawline — for balance and definition
  • Nasolabial and marionette lines — softening deeper folds
  • Hydration and subtle contouring

What happens at your appointment

Your consultation comes first: we discuss your goals, assess your face and confirm suitability. If it's right for you, the filler is placed precisely and comfortably, with regular checks on symmetry and shape. You'll leave with clear aftercare and a point of contact.

Aftercare

Mild swelling, tenderness or small bruising can happen and usually settles within a few days. Stay hydrated, avoid heavy exercise, alcohol and heat for 24 hours, and avoid pressing the area. Get in touch any time with questions.

Common questions

How long do dermal fillers last?

It varies by area and product, but results commonly last several months to over a year before gradually softening. Your practitioner will give you a realistic idea at your consultation.

Will it look natural?

That's the whole aim — a balanced, refreshed result agreed with you first. Eleana treats conservatively and reviews shape as she goes.

Does it hurt?

Most people find it very manageable. Many fillers contain a local anaesthetic and numbing can be used for comfort.

Is there a consultation first?

Yes — every treatment starts with a free, no-obligation consultation to check suitability.
