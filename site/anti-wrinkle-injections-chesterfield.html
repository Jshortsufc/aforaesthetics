Anti-wrinkle injections · Chesterfield

Anti-wrinkle injections for Chesterfield

Reviewed by Eleana, Advanced Nurse Practitioner & independent prescriber

Natural-looking softening of frown, forehead and eye lines for Chesterfield clients — assessed, prescribed and carried out by a nurse prescriber at two Sheffield clinics, a simple drive up the A61. Every treatment starts with a free consultation.

A simple drive from Chesterfield

Chesterfield is around twelve miles south of Sheffield, and the run up the A61 is about as direct as it gets. Whether you're setting off from the town centre, Brimington, Whittington, Walton or Hasland, most clients reach either clinic in roughly twenty-five to thirty-five minutes. It's an easy trip to make for treatment carried out by a nurse prescriber rather than a beauty counter.

We treat from two locations, so you can choose whichever is the easier run for you:

  • And Collective, Abbeydale Road — south Sheffield, the closest of the two coming up from Chesterfield and right on the main road in. Simple parking.
  • Peaches, Hillsborough — north-west Sheffield, handy if you're heading across the city or combining the trip with other plans. Easy parking and a tram stop nearby.

Full addresses and directions for both are on our where we are page.

Why Chesterfield clients choose a nurse prescriber

Anti-wrinkle injections use a prescription-only medicine, which by law must be prescribed by a suitably qualified prescriber after a face-to-face assessment — not sold like a beauty product. That's why clients are happy to make the trip up from Chesterfield. Your treatment is assessed, prescribed and carried out by Eleana, an Advanced Nurse Practitioner and independent prescriber, so you're in genuinely clinical hands throughout.

It also means honesty. If a treatment isn't right for you, or a lighter approach would suit you better, you'll be told plainly — with no upsell and no pressure to book on the day.

What the treatment involves

Anti-wrinkle injections relax the specific muscles that create certain expression lines, so the lines above them soften over the following week or two. Common areas are the frown lines between the brows, forehead lines and crow's feet, though a brow lift, gummy smile, jaw slimming for teeth grinding and excessive sweating can also be discussed. The aim at A for Aesthetics is always a fresher, more relaxed look — never frozen.

Your appointment begins with a consultation and assessment, where Eleana checks the treatment is suitable and agrees a conservative plan with you. Treatment itself is quick, and you'll get full aftercare advice with a review to check how it's settled.

Natural-looking results, agreed with you first

Plenty of people put off anti-wrinkle treatment because they've seen results that look overdone. That's not the approach here. Because everything is agreed at the consultation and kept deliberately subtle, the result is tailored to your face and your expressions rather than a one-size-fits-all. Many Chesterfield clients want nothing dramatic — just to soften the lines that have started to settle in, while still looking like themselves.

Common questions from Chesterfield clients

How far is it from Chesterfield?

Chesterfield is around twelve miles south of Sheffield, a straightforward run up the A61. Most clients reach either clinic in roughly twenty-five to thirty-five minutes, and parking is easy at both.

How long do results last?

Typically around three to four months before gradually softening. It varies from person to person, and Eleana will give you a realistic idea at your consultation.

Will I still be able to make expressions?

The aim is a natural, refreshed look rather than a frozen one. A conservative approach keeps movement while softening the lines that bother you — all agreed with you first.

Is there a consultation first?

Yes — it's a prescription-only treatment, so a free, face-to-face assessment with a nurse prescriber is required to confirm suitability before anything is arranged.
