Questions & answers

Frequently asked questions

Everything you might want to know before booking. If your question isn't here, just get in touch — we're happy to help.

About the clinic

Is A for Aesthetics nurse-led?

Yes. Every treatment is carried out by Eleana, an Advanced Nurse Practitioner, NMC-registered nurse and independent prescriber with a master's degree and 8 years in aesthetics.

Where are you based?

Our main clinic is at Peaches, 5 Wadsley Lane, Hillsborough, Sheffield S6 4EA, and we cover South Yorkshire including Rotherham, Barnsley, Doncaster and North Chesterfield.

Do you offer a consultation?

Yes — every client gets a free, no-obligation consultation. Treatments are only ever recommended when they're genuinely right for you.

About treatments

Will my results look natural?

That's always the aim. Eleana treats conservatively and tailors everything to your features — refreshed and natural, never overdone.

Do treatments hurt?

Most people find them very manageable. Numbing options are used for comfort, and many fillers contain a local anaesthetic.

How long do results last?

It depends on the treatment and the individual — anywhere from a few months to over a year. Your consultation will give you a realistic idea.

Are anti-wrinkle injections the same as Botox?

Botox is a well-known brand of botulinum toxin, the prescription-only medicine used in anti-wrinkle injections. Because it's prescription-only, UK rules mean it can't be advertised by brand — your practitioner will discuss specifics at your consultation.

Booking & preparing

How do I book?

Use the Book now button to book online through our booking system, or call or message us.

What's your cancellation policy?

Please give us as much notice as possible if you need to rearrange. Any deposit terms are shown at the time of booking.

How should I prepare?

For most treatments, avoid alcohol and blood-thinning supplements for 24 hours beforehand where you can. We'll confirm anything specific at your consultation.

Aftercare

What aftercare will I get?

Clear, tailored aftercare for your specific treatment, plus a point of contact if you have any questions afterwards.

Is some swelling or bruising normal?

Yes — mild, temporary swelling, tenderness or small bruising can happen with injectable treatments and usually settles within a few days.

Still have a question?

Book a free consultation or drop us a message — Eleana is happy to talk anything through with no pressure.
