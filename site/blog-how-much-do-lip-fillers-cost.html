How much do lip fillers cost?

If you're weighing up lip filler, cost is usually one of the first questions. Here's an honest look at what you can expect to pay in the UK and here in Sheffield, what actually drives the price, and why the cheapest option is rarely the safe one.

What affects the price of lip fillers?

There isn't a single national price for lip filler because several things feed into it. Understanding them helps you compare like for like rather than just chasing the lowest number.

  • Amount of product (ml). Filler is measured in millilitres. A subtle refresh using 0.5ml costs less than a full 1ml treatment. More product means more cost, but more is not always better — the right amount depends on your lips and your goals.
  • Practitioner expertise. A treatment carried out by an experienced medical professional who can assess your face, inject safely and manage any complication is not the same service as a quick appointment with an untrained injector, even if the end product looks similar on paper.
  • Brand of filler. Reputable clinics use well-established, regulated hyaluronic acid dermal fillers. Quality products cost more to buy in, and that's reflected in the price.
  • Location and clinic. Prices tend to be higher in central London than in much of the North. Sheffield and South Yorkshire sit comfortably within the typical UK range rather than at the top of it.

Typical UK lip filler prices

As a general guide, lip filler in the UK usually costs somewhere in the region of £150 to £300 per treatment. Half-syringe (0.5ml) treatments sit towards the lower end, and a full 1ml treatment towards the middle of that range. These are broad figures, not a quote — the only way to know your price is a proper consultation and assessment.

At A for Aesthetics, lip filler starts from £150 for 0.5ml and £200 for 1ml. You can see current pricing in full on our prices page, and read more about the treatment itself on our lip fillers page.

Why "cheap" lip fillers are a red flag

It's tempting to book the lowest price you can find, but very cheap lip filler is one of the clearest warning signs in this industry. A price well below the typical range often means one or more corners have been cut: a less experienced injector, an unbranded or unknown product, or no real consultation, medical history check or aftercare.

Lip filler is a medical procedure. Complications are uncommon, but when they happen — for example a blocked blood vessel — they need to be recognised and treated quickly by someone qualified to prescribe. At A for Aesthetics your treatment is carried out by Eleana, an Advanced Nurse Practitioner and independent prescriber, which means the person treating you is also the person able to manage a complication safely. That clinical safety net is a large part of what you're paying for, and it's not something a bargain price can offer.

What's included in the price?

A fair price should cover more than the few minutes of injecting. At A for Aesthetics it includes:

  • A free, no-obligation consultation before any treatment, so you're assessed properly and given honest advice — including being told if lip filler isn't right for you.
  • The treatment itself, using a quality regulated dermal filler.
  • Clear aftercare guidance and a point of contact if you have any concerns afterwards.

Every treatment starts with that free consultation for a reason: it lets us understand what you want, check it's suitable and set realistic expectations before you commit to anything.

Getting a price that's right for you

The honest answer to "how much do lip fillers cost?" is: it depends on your lips, your goals and who you trust to do the work. Rather than booking on price alone, look for a qualified, prescribing practitioner, a clear consultation and a price that sits sensibly within the UK range. If you'd like a personalised idea of cost, the best next step is a free consultation — you can get in touch to arrange one.
